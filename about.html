Curious mind. Holistic thinker. Relentlessly improving.

Hi! I’m Michael, a Dutch product designer based in Tokyo and I am driven by solving complex problems and turning these into simple and easy-to-use solutions.

Currently, I'm a Design Manager at PayPay, one of Japan's fastest-growing fintech companies. Previously, I led design at Japan Travel, a full-stack inbound travel & media agency, and at Cream, a digital agency in Lausanne and Tokyo. Across these roles, I've designed a wide range of digital products –  from training platforms to media-rich travel experiences – for diverse audiences and industries.

Working across startups and cross-functional teams has shaped how I move fast, think holistically, and bridge user needs with business goals. I’m passionate about crafting accessible, thoughtful, and delightful experiences that create lasting value.

Outside of work, I start most mornings with a quality cup of coffee. On weekends, you'll often find me reading, exploring cafes, or discovering new places. I also enjoy connecting with Tokyo’s international design community through local UX meetups.

Let’s chat over some coffee. — Michael
